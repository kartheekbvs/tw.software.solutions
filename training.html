Weekly Quizzes & Progress Tracking

The TWSS Training module helps you reinforce what you've learned through structured weekly quizzes. Each course comes with multiple weeks of curated questions designed to test your understanding of core concepts, from programming fundamentals to advanced topics.

Track your progress over time with detailed score history, attempt tracking, and visual indicators for completed, in-progress, and locked modules. Quizzes are scored out of 5, and you need at least 4 correct answers (80%) to mark a week as complete. You can retake any quiz to improve your score.

Training content is unlocked based on your purchased courses. Buy a course from our catalog to gain access to its full training program, including weekly quizzes and progress tracking for C, C++, Java, Python, and SQL.

What Each Training Track Covers

C Language Training

8 weeks covering variables and data types, loops and conditional logic, arrays and strings, functions and recursion, pointers and memory addresses, structures and typedef, dynamic memory allocation with malloc and calloc, and file handling with fopen and fclose. Each week includes 5 quiz questions to reinforce core C programming concepts.

C++ Language Training

8 weeks covering iostream and namespaces, classes and objects, inheritance and polymorphism, encapsulation and abstraction, templates and the STL, exception handling, operator overloading and copy semantics, and smart pointers with C++11 features. Master object-oriented programming and modern C++ practices.

JAVA Language Training

8 weeks covering JVM fundamentals and main method, primitive types and the String class, inheritance and method overriding, interfaces and abstract classes, collections framework with ArrayList and HashMap, exception handling with try-catch-finally, multithreading and synchronization, and JDBC database connectivity. Build enterprise-grade Java applications.

Python Language Training

8 weeks covering print and variable declaration, lists slicing and dictionaries, functions with def and lambda, classes with __init__ and inheritance, file handling and exception management, modules and virtual environments, list comprehensions generators and decorators, and data science libraries including pandas, NumPy, and matplotlib. From basics to advanced Python engineering.

SQL Language Training

6 weeks covering SELECT statements and WHERE clauses, JOIN operations including INNER JOIN and LEFT JOIN, GROUP BY and aggregate functions, subqueries and correlated subqueries, normalization and primary key and foreign key constraints, and indexing views and stored procedures. Master database querying and management from beginner to advanced level.
