Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our courses, payments, and platform features. If you need further assistance, feel free to reach out using the form above.

How do I access my purchased courses?

After completing your purchase, log in to your Dashboard using the same email address you used during checkout. All your purchased courses will appear in your library with direct access links. You can also use the "Verify Payment" button on the Dashboard if your courses are not showing up immediately after a successful payment.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept payments through Razorpay, which supports UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), credit cards, debit cards, and net banking. All transactions are secured with industry-standard encryption. If you experience any payment issues, our support team is available to assist you within 24 hours.

Do courses include lifetime access?

Yes, every course purchase comes with lifetime access. Once you buy a course, it remains available in your Dashboard permanently. There are no recurring fees or subscription charges. You can revisit the content as many times as you need, and any updates to the course material are included at no additional cost.

What is the difference between Notes and Plans?

Notes are individual course materials focused on a single programming language or technology, such as C, C++, Java, Python, or SQL. Plans are comprehensive bundles that combine multiple courses with additional resources like DSA training, full-stack development, data analytics platforms, or a complete 4-year college curriculum package. Plans offer the best value for learners who want a structured learning path.

How do I take exams and get certified?

Visit the Exam Portal to select your course and start testing your skills. Our exams are powered by SpeedExam and cover topics like C, C++, Java, Python, JavaScript, DBMS, Data Structures, Operating Systems, and Networking. After completing an exam, you receive detailed performance feedback and can earn verified certificates that demonstrate your competency to employers.

I paid but my courses are not showing. What should I do?

This can happen with UPI payments where the payment app may not return you to the website properly. Go to your Dashboard and click the "Verify Payment" button. Enter your Razorpay Payment ID (found in your bank statement, starts with "pay_"), and our system will locate and link your purchase. If you still face issues, email us at support@twss.shop with your payment details and we will resolve it within 24 hours.
